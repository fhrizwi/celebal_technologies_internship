Q22 — LEFT JOIN vs RIGHT JOIN
LEFT JOIN

A LEFT JOIN:

-Returns all records from the left table.
-Returns matching records from the right table.
-Displays NULL for non-matching rows.


Example--
SELECT *
FROM customers c
LEFT JOIN orders o
ON c.customer_id=o.customer_id;

Here:
-All customers will appear.
-Customers without orders will show NULL values.

RIGHT JOIN-------------------------------

A RIGHT JOIN:
-Returns all records from the right table.

Example--
SELECT *
FROM customers c
RIGHT JOIN orders o
ON c.customer_id=o.customer_id;


FULL OUTER JOIN------------------------

A FULL OUTER JOIN:
-Returns all records from both tables.
-Displays NULL where matches do not exist.

Use Case--------------
It is useful for identifying missing relationships between tables.
