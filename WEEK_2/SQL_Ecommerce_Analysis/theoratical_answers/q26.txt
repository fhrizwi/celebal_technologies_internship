Q26 — ACID Properties
A — Atomicity
Meaning--------------
A transaction is completed fully or rolled back completely.

Example--------------
In a bank transfer:

-Money deducted from one account
-Must also be added to another account
-Partial transactions are not allowed.


C — Consistency
Meaning------------
The database must always remain valid according to defined rules.

Example-----------------
Negative balances should not be allowed


I — Isolation
Meaning-------------
Multiple transactions should not interfere with each other.

Example--------------
Two users purchasing the same product simultaneously.

D — Durability
Meaning----------------
Once committed, data remains permanently saved.

Example--------------
Even after a system crash, committed transactions remain
